How to play

  1. Pick your slope. Choose one of ten run types. Clear 24 of 30 gates on Levels 1–3, 26 on Levels 4–6, and 28 on Levels 7–10. Continue into Expert stages after Level 10.
  2. Drag to steer. Ski between the flags and dodge rocks, trees and other skiers.
  3. Watch the finish. Animals cross near the final flags. Retry replays the same layout. New Layout gives you a fresh one.

Privacy policy

Updated 22 November 2026

You can play Keith Goes Skiing offline without creating an account. Scores, unlocked levels, sound and analytics preferences are stored on your device. When Usage analytics is enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app activity, session information, device and app information, and an app instance identifier to help us understand usage and improve the game. Events include run type, level, score, gates cleared, collision reason, retries and sharing actions. Google can derive an approximate location from network information. You can turn collection off with Usage analytics on the main menu. We disable advertising ID collection and ad personalization signals. Internet access is needed to send analytics.

Sharing opens your device’s share sheet. Nothing is posted until you choose a recipient or app and send it. A score card contains your run type, level, score and gates cleared. A challenge link also contains a layout seed and game rules version so a friend can play the same run. Recipients can read and forward this information. The social or messaging service you select handles it under its own policy. This website uses the challenge code in the link to display the challenge.

We use Google Analytics to measure visits to this website and understand how people find and use it. We remove friend challenge codes from page addresses sent to Analytics. Our hosting provider may process ordinary request information when you open the website.

You can remove locally stored game information by clearing the app’s data where your device provides that option, or by removing the app. If you email us for support, we use the information you provide to respond and resolve your request. We retain the correspondence as needed for those purposes and any applicable obligations. You can email us to request access to or deletion of your support correspondence.

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date. For privacy questions, email us.

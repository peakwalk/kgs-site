How to play

  1. Pick your slope. Choose one of ten run types. Clear 24 of 30 gates on Levels 1–3, 26 on Levels 4–6, and 28 on Levels 7–10. Continue into Expert stages after Level 10.
  2. Drag to steer. Ski between the flags and dodge rocks, trees and other skiers.
  3. Watch the finish. Animals cross near the final flags. Retry replays the same layout. New Layout gives you a fresh one.

Keith Goes Skiing Privacy Policy

Updated 22 November 2026

Playing the game. You can play offline without an account. The game stores unlocked levels, personal best scores, and sound and language settings on your device. To remove these records, delete the app or clear its storage if your device offers that option.

Sharing scores and challenges. When you share, your device’s share sheet lets you choose a recipient or app. A score card includes the run type, level, score and gates cleared. A challenge link also includes information used to recreate the course and show the score to beat. Recipients can read and forward the link. The messaging or social service you choose handles shared information under its own policy. The website reads the code from the link to display the challenge. You can also paste a challenge code directly into the game.

Website hosting. This website uses HTTPS and is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security. When you open a challenge link, GitHub Pages receives the requested web address, which includes the challenge code. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its data handling practices.

Website analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this website, including the pages they visit and interactions such as scrolling. Google Analytics uses browser cookies to recognise browsers and sessions. It receives technical information about the browser and device, and uses request IP addresses to estimate visitors’ general location. We remove challenge codes from page addresses sent to Analytics. The Analytics property is configured to retain event data for 2 months and user data for 14 months. A new visit can reset the user data retention period. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Support and privacy requests. If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Microsoft handles incoming email for our support domain. We use your correspondence to respond to your request and retain it as needed to handle the issue and meet applicable obligations. To ask about retention or request access to or deletion of your support correspondence, email our support address.
